Why These Thresholds?

Credit risk governance principles and institutional rationale

1
Empirical Default Spread: Calibrated default rates show a >9.7x divergence between Low (2.67%) and High (25.90%) risk tiers, ensuring maximum separation.
2
Straight-Through Processing (STP) Efficiency: 50.9% of total applications qualify for automated fast-track approval, dramatically slashing underwriting turnaround times.
3
Targeted Loss Mitigation: Isolates over 43% of all delinquent debt into the strict underwriting tier without unnecessarily burdening prime borrowers.
Regulatory Compliance & Decision-Support Notice

These heuristic policy rules and ML risk bands function strictly as ML-derived decision-support guardrails for underwriting review. They do not constitute statutory or regulatory credit policy mandates and must be reviewed alongside human underwriter discretion.
